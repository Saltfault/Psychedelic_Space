## Asset organization

The curated GameAssets packs are organized by use rather than kept in a catch-all folder:

- Ship sheets and ships: assets/sprites/ships/
- Enemies: assets/sprites/enemies/
- Projectiles, pickups, and effects: assets/sprites/projectiles/, assets/sprites/pickups/, and assets/sprites/effects/
- Planets, moons, structures, and world art: assets/sprites/world/
- Interface art and card art: assets/ui/
- Pixelosopher font files: assets/fonts/
- Pixel UI Sci-Fi Free addon assets: addons/pixel_ui_scifi/

Each imported pack retains its own CREDIT.txt beside its files. Pixel UI Sci-Fi Free's credit is kept with its addon, and Pixelosopher's credit is kept with the installed font. Several packs explicitly mark their source or license as unverified; check each pack's credit/license terms before using it in a public release. The imported files are not all wired into game scenes, and standalone pack redistribution may be prohibited by the included terms.
