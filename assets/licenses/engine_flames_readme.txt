ENGINE FLAMES — Version 1.0
By Dolkyns 

INCLUDED
3 flame types, 18 color variants.
Transparent PNG frames, grid and strip spritesheets, GIF previews.

SPECIFICATIONS
Type 1: 256 × 256 px | 16 frames | 24 FPS | Origin: [128, 22]
Type 2: 256 × 256 px | 16 frames | 24 FPS | Origin: [128, 34]
Type 3: 256 × 256 px | 16 frames | 24 FPS | Origin: [128, 29]

Origin coordinates are recommended pixel positions measured from the top-left corner of each frame.

USAGE
Play frames in numerical order and enable looping.
Read spritesheet grids left to right, then top to bottom.
Flames point downward, with the attachment at the top.
GIFs are previews; use the PNG assets in your project.
